Privacy

Last updated November 2026.

TrueCountLab is a free practice tool for blackjack card counting. It uses play money only: there's no gambling, no real money and no ads. This page explains what it stores and why.

If you play as a guest

Your settings and progress are saved in your own browser (local storage) and aren't sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

If you create an account

  • Account details: your display name and email address. Your display name is shown to other players on tables and leaderboards; your email is not.
  • Password: stored only as a salted scrypt hash. We never store or see the password itself.
  • Google or Discord sign-in: if you use it, we keep that service's account ID and the email it shares, so you can sign in again. We don't get your password for those services or access to anything else in them.
  • Practice data: your settings, drill results and stats, simulator bankroll and sessions, and any casino sessions you log yourself.

We use your email only to sign you in and to send password-reset emails you ask for. We don't sell or share your data, and we don't send marketing email.

Playing online

  • Guests pick a nickname. It and a random ID are kept in your browser so you can rejoin a room; nothing about guests is stored on our server.
  • Signed-in players: we keep your count battle rating, wins and accuracy, and your shared-table accuracy (plays, bets and count checks). Your display name and these numbers can appear on the public leaderboards.
  • Chat is passed live to the people in your room and isn't saved. The last few messages stay in the room's memory until the room closes, so people who join can catch up. Slurs and strong profanity are masked, and room hosts can remove players.
  • Rooms and games exist only while people are in them.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in (tcl_session). A short-lived cookie is used while you sign in with Google or Discord, to make sure the sign-in was started by you. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Analytics and hosting

The site is served through Cloudflare. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits without cookies and without tracking individual people. Account data is stored on our own server.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Account page → Download my data gives you everything saved to your account as a file.
  • Delete your account: Account page → Delete account permanently removes your account, sign-in connections, saved progress and online stats (taking you off the leaderboards).
